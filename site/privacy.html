Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

The short version

Desktop Buddy keeps your data on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on a feature that needs the internet, and each one is listed below.

Stored on your computer

  • Tasks, reminders, habits, notes, bookmarks, settings and your buddy's progress are stored in a local database in your Windows user folder.
  • Uninstalling the app leaves this data in place so a reinstall picks up where you left off. You can export it any time from Settings → Privacy & data.

Sent only if you use the feature

  • Cloud sync (off by default): your email address, a hashed password and your tasks are stored on our sync server so your computers stay in step. Bookmarks and notes are never synced. You can delete your account and all synced tasks from Account & sync.
  • Feedback: when you send feedback, we receive your message, the type you chose, your app and Windows version, and your email only if you enter one.
  • Crash reports (off by default): if you turn them on, errors are sent with the error message, the code location, and your app and Windows version. Folder names are removed. No tasks, notes or files are included.
  • Weather: the location you pick is sent to Open-Meteo (and to OpenStreetMap when searching for a place) to get the forecast.
  • AI chat: if you add your own Anthropic API key, the messages you type in Chat are sent to Anthropic using your key.

Always on

  • Updates: the app checks GitHub for new versions. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any download.
  • Announcements: the app occasionally downloads short news messages from our server. No information about you is sent.

What we never do

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and no selling or sharing of data.
  • We never read the contents of your screen or other apps, except for the text capture shortcut, which runs only when you press it and keeps the result on your PC.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: use Send feedback in the app or open an issue on GitHub.
